Method

Shape versus accuracy: raising cutoff to ~20 and anti-blob to ~0.8 keeps coastlines straighter and regions less circular, but costs area accuracy and speed. Worth it for a dozen large regions, not for hundreds of small ones.

Each pass re-rasterizes the current map and halves the blur. Small regions need a finer grid: they are only as accurate as the cells they cover, and a region smaller than one cell can only be approximated. Watch the under-resolved warning — for NUTS 3, 512 or 1024.
